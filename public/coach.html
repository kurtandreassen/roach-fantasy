Roach Coach

No live CBS API exists, so nothing here auto-pulls from CBS. The real sync happens by asking Claude to read your open Roster / Waivers / Standings tabs (via Claude-in-Chrome) and push the update — this dashboard just shows the current synced state, persisted server-side, until the next update.

Roster & Lineup

One player per line: Name, POS, score (score = weekly projection or any relative ranking, higher = better). Ask Claude to fill this from your open CBS Roster tab, or paste it yourself.


Waiver Suggestions

Available free agents (one name per line, must match the board). Ask Claude to fill this from your open CBS Free Agents tab, or paste it yourself.


Standings

One team per line: Team, W-L-T, Points For. Ask Claude to fill this from your open CBS Standings tab, or paste it yourself.
